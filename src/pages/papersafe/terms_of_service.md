**Your backups**

PaperSafe works hard to keep every item it can see in your library backed up, but no backup is guaranteed. Items in your Hidden album are not backed up, nor are files larger than Telegram allows, and if you allow access to only some photos, only those are backed up. Backups need a working internet connection and enough space on your iPhone, and they depend on iOS, your photo library and Telegram. Check the Backup Status screen in the app to see what is backed up. I'm not responsible for any loss of photos, videos or other data, however caused, to the extent the law allows.

Deleting an item on your iPhone never deletes its backup in Telegram. PaperSafe deletes only its own outdated copies of photo edits and previews that it uploaded, and uploads it cancels before they finish; it never deletes a backed-up original. Backups you want removed must be deleted in Telegram. If the item is still on your iPhone and Backup is on, PaperSafe uploads it again, so delete it from your iPhone first or turn Backup off.

If you switch to another backup chat, the old copies stay in the previous chat until you delete them in Telegram.

If you back up to a channel or group that other people can see, they can see your photos and videos. Choosing that chat is your decision.

**Data charges**

Backups can use a lot of data. By default, PaperSafe waits for Wi-Fi before it starts an upload. If you turn on Use Cellular Data in Settings, your mobile operator's charges apply, including roaming.

Even with that setting off, an upload already under way when you leave Wi-Fi can finish over cellular data, and viewing, sharing or saving items from Telegram or iCloud uses whatever connection you are on.
