**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to tgcloud ("the app"), an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac built by Sep Behroozi ("I", "me"). tgcloud is a cloud drive that keeps your files and folders in a Telegram chat in your own Telegram account.

**The short version**

tgcloud has no servers of its own. I don't collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting services. Your files go only from your device to your own Telegram account.

**Telegram**

tgcloud uses the Telegram API through TDLib, Telegram's official client library. It connects directly to Telegram's servers. When you use it:

*   Your phone number, the login code Telegram sends you and, if you have one, your two-step verification password are sent to Telegram to sign you in. If you sign in with a QR code, the code is shown on your screen and scanned by Telegram on another device. None of this is ever sent to me.
*   The files you add are uploaded, unchanged, to the Telegram chat you choose: Saved Messages, or a private group or channel of yours. Images and videos also get a small preview image.
*   Each file message, and each folder, has a short technical caption that tgcloud uses to rebuild your drive: an identifier for the item, the folder it is in, its name, its size, a fingerprint of its contents, its modification date and, while it is in Trash, the time it was trashed.
*   Files are ordinary Telegram documents. tgcloud doesn't encrypt them, and they can be opened from any Telegram app signed in to your account.

What Telegram does with this data is covered by [Telegram's Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy). Anyone who can see the chat your drive is in can see your files. tgcloud only accepts a chat you own that has no public link.

**Data stored on your device**

To work, tgcloud keeps some data on your device only:

*   Your Telegram session and a cache managed by TDLib.
*   A drive database: the list of your files and folders, rebuilt from your Telegram chat whenever needed.
*   Files you open or keep downloaded, and small preview images.
*   Your recent files and your settings, such as which chat holds your drive.

Signing out in tgcloud deletes the Telegram session, the drive database and every downloaded file from your device; your files stay in Telegram. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

**Files, Finder and the share sheet**

tgcloud shows your drive in the Files app on iPhone and iPad and in Finder on the Mac, and lets you save to it from other apps' share sheets. Files you place there, or share to tgcloud, are uploaded to your Telegram chat. tgcloud reads only the files you give it.

**Deleting your files**

Deleting a file or folder moves it to tgcloud's Trash; it stays in Telegram. Emptying the Trash, or deleting an item from it, deletes its messages from Telegram. You can also delete the messages in Telegram, or delete your Telegram account, as described by Telegram.

**Children's privacy**

tgcloud is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Using Telegram is subject to Telegram's own age requirements.
