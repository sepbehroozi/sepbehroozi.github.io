**Contact**

For help with tgcloud, or to report a problem, email efromfb@gmail.com. Please include your device model, its iOS, iPadOS or macOS version and what you see in the app's Uploads screen.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Where are my files?**

In the Telegram chat you chose when you signed in: Saved Messages, or a private group or channel of yours. You can see the current chat in Settings, under Storage.

**Why can't I see tgcloud in Finder or the Files app?**

New locations start turned off. On the Mac, open System Settings, then General, Login Items & Extensions, File Providers, and turn on tgcloud. On iPhone and iPad, open the Files app, tap Browse, then the More button and Edit, and turn on tgcloud.

**If I delete a file, is it deleted from Telegram?**

Not until you empty the Trash. Deleting moves a file or folder to tgcloud's Trash, where you can restore it. The Trash never empties itself.

**Is there a storage limit?**

There is no limit on the total size of your drive. Each file can be up to 2 GB, or 4 GB with Telegram Premium. Larger files are refused with a message.

**Why isn't a file uploaded yet?**

Open Uploads. It shows what is uploading and what is waiting, for example because uploads are paused, Telegram isn't reachable, or Telegram asked to wait.

**Can I use my drive on another device?**

Yes. Sign in to tgcloud with the same Telegram account and choose the same chat. Your whole drive comes back.

**Can I move my drive to another chat?**

You can switch chats in Settings, under Storage. Switching shows that chat's drive; nothing is moved between chats.

**Is tgcloud made by Telegram?**

No. tgcloud is an independent app that uses the Telegram API and is not affiliated with Telegram.
